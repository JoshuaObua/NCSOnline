NATIONAL ATHLETE MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM (NAMIS)
The objective is to create a centralized national athlete management system called National Athlete Management Information System (NAMIS) that tracks athlete profiles, performance, medals, federations, coaches, talent pathways, safeguarding, and national team participation. Since sports in Uganda are organized through recognized National Federations and Associations, the database should be linked to federations and athlete registrations.
