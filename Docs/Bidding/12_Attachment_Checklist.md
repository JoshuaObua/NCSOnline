## Final Compilation Checks

- All pages numbered sequentially.
- All required forms signed and stamped.
- One original and two copies prepared, unless NCS instructs otherwise.
- Original clearly marked `ORIGINAL`.
- Copies clearly marked `COPY`.
- Envelope sealed and labelled correctly.
- Submission made before the stated deadline.
