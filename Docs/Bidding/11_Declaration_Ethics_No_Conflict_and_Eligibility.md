# Declaration of Ethics, Eligibility and No Conflict of Interest

**Applicant:** Ateni Media Technologies  
**Procurement Entity:** National Council of Sports  
**Reference:** NCS/SERV/0013 - Provision of Website Design and Maintenance

## Eligibility Declaration

Ateni Media Technologies declares that:

- The company has the legal capacity to enter into a contract.
- The company is not insolvent, bankrupt, in receivership or being wound up.
- The company's business activities have not been suspended.
- The company is not subject to legal proceedings that would prevent performance of the contract.
- The company has fulfilled, or is in the process of fulfilling, applicable tax and statutory obligations.
- The company has not been suspended from participating in public procurement.

## No Conflict of Interest Declaration

Ateni Media Technologies declares that:

- It has no conflict of interest in relation to this application.
- It has not participated in preparing the specifications for this procurement in a way that would create an unfair advantage.
- It does not have a relationship with any other applicant or official that would improperly influence this procurement.
- It will immediately notify National Council of Sports of any material change that may affect this declaration.

## Ethical Conduct Declaration

Ateni Media Technologies undertakes to:

- Observe the highest standards of ethics during the procurement process and contract execution.
- Avoid corrupt, fraudulent, collusive or coercive practices.
- Provide truthful and verifiable information.
- Cooperate with lawful verification requests by National Council of Sports.
- Comply with the Code of Ethical Conduct for Providers.

## Commissions, Gratuities or Fees

No commissions, gratuities or improper fees have been paid or will be paid in relation to this application or any resulting contract.
