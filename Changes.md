Compliance Automation
The system should automatically:
✅ Send reminder emails before deadlines
✅ Flag overdue reports
✅ Generate federation compliance scores
✅ Rank federations by performance
✅ Generate NCS Board reports
✅ Generate annual sports statistics
✅ Maintain a live national athlete database
This approach would provide NCS with a real-time national sports information system, allowing management to monitor federations, athletes, medals, governance compliance, financial accountability, and sports development across Uganda from a single online platform.
